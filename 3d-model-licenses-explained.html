3D Model Licenses Explained: Personal, Commercial and Creative Commons

Anyone who downloads or sells a 3D model is bound by its licence. A clear licence protects the designer, and it tells buyers what they may do. This page explains the usual terms in plain language. It is general information, not legal advice.

Why licences matter

Copyright applies to a 3D design by default. If a creator gives no licence, others have no clear permission to print, share or sell it. Listing the terms avoids disputes and support emails.

Common terms

  • Personal use. You may print the model for yourself and give prints away, but not sell them.
  • Commercial use. You may sell physical prints, usually with limits on how many or on the platform.
  • Redistribution. Whether you may share the files themselves. Usually this is not allowed without permission.
  • Remix or derivative. Whether you may modify the design and share the result.
  • Attribution. Whether you must credit the original designer.

Creative Commons at a glance

LicenceMeaning in short
CC BYAnyone may use, modify and sell, if they credit the creator
CC BY-SAAs CC BY, but remixes must carry the same licence
CC BY-NCCredit required, no commercial use
CC BY-NDCredit required, no modified versions shared
CC0No rights reserved, free to use for any purpose

Writing your terms on a listing

  1. State whether prints may be sold and under what conditions.
  2. Say whether the files may be shared or uploaded elsewhere.
  3. Explain what credit you expect, if any.
  4. Mention remix rules.
  5. Keep the wording short and put it near the download button.

When you show previews

If you publish a preview video or image of someone else's model, check that the licence allows it. Many designers permit previews with credit, but not all do. For your own models, a preview with your name or logo helps people find the original.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell prints of a model I downloaded for free?

Only if its licence allows commercial use. Many free models are personal-use only.

Does the converter add a licence to my model?

No. It does not change your files. See How to Write a 3D Model Listing That Sells for where to place licence text.
