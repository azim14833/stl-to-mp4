How we write these guides

Each guide aims to be useful on its own, with concrete numbers and checklists that you can apply the same day. Values such as densities, bitrates and tolerances are typical figures; printers, filaments and platforms differ, so confirm them against your own equipment and the current rules of the sites you use. When we learn something that changes a recommendation, we update the guide and its date.
