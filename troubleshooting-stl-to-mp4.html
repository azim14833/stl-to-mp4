Troubleshooting STL to MP4: Black Frames, Slow Renders and Files That Will Not Play

Browser-based rendering depends on your device, so problems usually have a short list of causes. Work through the section that matches your symptom.

The model is black or looks flat

  • STL files carry no colour. Choose a material colour in the settings.
  • If faces look inside out, the file may have flipped normals. Repair it in your slicer or modelling tool.
  • Very dark colour plus a dark background hides detail. Raise the light strength or lighten the scene.

The model is tiny, cut off or off-centre

  • Check the up axis. Models exported with Z up often appear lying down. Use the orientation buttons to rotate it.
  • A single stray triangle far from the model can enlarge its bounding volume. Delete loose fragments in the source file.
  • Use the zoom slider and the vertical position slider to adjust framing after the automatic fit.
  • On phones, choose 4:5 or 9:16 so the preview uses the whole screen.

The model does not sit on the table in a photo scene

Set shadows on, use a camera elevation of about 18 to 24 degrees and adjust the Model size in the scene and vertical position sliders. If your own photo was taken from a very different angle, choose one of the studio scenes instead.

The export is very slow or stops

  • Lower the resolution or frame rate. 30 frames per second at 1080p is enough for most uses.
  • Shorten the duration. A camera tour needs 10 seconds or more, a turntable can be 5 to 8.
  • Close other tabs and keep the screen awake; mobile browsers slow down background tabs.
  • Decimate meshes with millions of triangles before loading them.

The page says the file is too large or fails to load

Try a smaller file, or export to a lower triangle count. Phones have much less memory than computers, so a model that works on a desktop may fail on a phone.

The MP4 will not play or will not upload

  • Some platforms reject very high bitrates. Use the size limit option to keep the file under the platform's cap.
  • If your browser produced WebM instead of MP4, convert it with a desktop video tool, or use a current Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  • Check the aspect ratio expected by the platform; a wrong ratio can cause black bars or rejection.

Colours look different from the preview

The preview uses lower quality shadows and may skip background blur. The export applies them. Judge the look from a snapshot or a short test export.
