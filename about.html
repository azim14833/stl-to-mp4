About STLtoMP4

STLtoMP4.com is a free online tool that turns 3D models into MP4 turntable videos and a set of view images. It was built for makers, designers and 3D printing sellers who need good-looking previews quickly, without installing a renderer or learning a complicated program.

What the tool does

  • Opens STL (binary and ASCII), 3MF, OBJ and PLY files, including coloured models.
  • Shows the model's size, volume, surface area and an estimated PLA weight, with a choice of file unit.
  • Lets you set the scene, material, colour, camera angle and lighting.
  • Exports an MP4 turntable video (resolution, frame rate and bitrate are adjustable), numbered view images in PNG, JPG or WebP, and optionally a GIF and a contact sheet.
  • Works in seven interface languages and can be installed as an app that works offline after the first visit.

Why we built it

Presenting a 3D print well usually needs rendering software, time and powerful hardware. We wanted a fast, simple alternative that runs on any modern computer or phone, keeps your files private and costs nothing. The guides section grew from the questions that makers keep running into: wrong scale, flat lighting, oversized files, unclear listings.

How it works and how your files are treated

Everything happens in your browser using WebGL and WebCodecs. Your model is never uploaded to our servers, so private or unreleased designs stay on your device. Video encoding depends on your browser and hardware, so maximum resolution can vary between devices. Our privacy policy explains what is stored locally and how advertising works.

How the site is funded

The tool is free for personal and commercial use. The site is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Ads are kept separate from the guides, and we do not accept payment to change what a guide recommends.

Our approach to guides

Guides are written to help you decide and act: they explain why a problem happens, give numbers you can check and end with checklists. We avoid copying other websites, we label estimates as estimates, and we correct mistakes when readers point them out. Read more on the guides page.
