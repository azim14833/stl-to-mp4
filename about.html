About STLtoMP4

STLtoMP4.com is a free online tool that converts 3D models into MP4 turntable videos and view images. It was created for makers, designers and 3D printing sellers who need good-looking previews quickly.

Why we built it

Presenting a 3D print well usually needs rendering software, time and powerful hardware. We wanted a fast, simple alternative that runs on any modern computer or phone, keeps your files private and costs nothing.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser using WebGL and WebCodecs. Your STL, 3MF or OBJ file is never uploaded to our servers. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps the tool free.
