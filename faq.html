STLtoMP4 FAQ: Common Questions Answered

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. For longer explanations see the guides.

Is STLtoMP4 free?

Yes. It is free for personal and commercial use.

Are my 3D files uploaded?

No. Files are read and rendered inside your browser and never sent to a server. See how it works.

Which file formats can I open?

STL (binary and ASCII), OBJ, 3MF and PLY.

What is the camera tour?

A video in which the camera moves to several angles around the model: low, high, sideways and close-up. By default it is exported as a second video next to the turntable. See camera angles for 3D model videos.

Why does my model look too big or too small in a scene?

Use the Model size in the scene slider, the Zoom slider and the vertical position slider. Phones work best with 4:5 or 9:16 output.

Why does the model not sit on the table in a photo scene?

Turn shadows on and use a camera elevation of about 18 to 24 degrees. See choosing a scene.

Which video size should I choose?

16:9 for websites and marketplaces, 4:5 for Instagram posts and 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Does the video have sound?

No. The converter creates silent video; add music in an editor or in the platform.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. Rendering is local, so it can be slower than on a computer. Close other tabs and keep the screen on.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. Use Install app in your browser menu. It then works offline after the first visit.

How do I fix a model that lies on its side?

Use the orientation buttons. See this guide.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the contact page.
