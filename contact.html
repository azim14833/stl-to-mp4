Reporting a bug

To help us reproduce the problem, please include:

  • Your browser and version, and your device (computer, phone or tablet).
  • The file format (STL, 3MF, OBJ or PLY) and roughly how large the file is.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  • The export settings you used: resolution, frame rate and bitrate.

We never need your 3D files to help you. Please do not send private or confidential models by email.

Feature ideas and feedback

Tell us what you are trying to make: a listing video, a social post, a catalogue sheet. Knowing the goal helps us decide which features are worth adding.

Privacy and legal requests

For questions about data, advertising or your rights under GDPR or the Turkish KVKK, see the privacy policy and write to the address above. For copyright concerns about content on this site, include the page address and a short description of the issue.

Response time

We normally reply within a few working days.
