Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Your 3D files

STLtoMP4.com processes your STL, 3MF and OBJ files entirely in your browser. They are not uploaded, stored or seen by us.

The site stores your preferences (language, theme, render settings) in your browser's local storage and caches the app files so it can work offline. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Turkey) a consent message lets you choose.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and request time) for security and operation.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is usually nothing to delete. For questions or requests under GDPR or the Turkish KVKK, contact info@stltomp4.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
