Best Image Sizes and Views for 3D Print Listings and Social Media

Good presentation sells 3D models. Two designs of equal quality will not get equal attention if one is shown with a blurry, badly cropped picture. This guide explains which views buyers expect and which sizes suit common destinations.

The views buyers expect

A complete set tells a buyer everything about the shape: a shaded perspective view as the hero image, then front, back, left and right views, a top view, a bottom view, and an orthographic view that shows true proportions without perspective distortion. A wireframe image is a useful extra because it reveals mesh density and printability. Showing the bottom matters more than most sellers think, since it proves the model has a flat base and no hidden problems.

Aspect ratios by destination

  • Marketplace main image: 16:9 or 4:3 landscape, around 1920 x 1080, unless the platform specifies otherwise. Always read the upload rules of the site you use, because limits change.
  • Instagram feed: 1:1, 1080 x 1080, or 4:5 portrait.
  • Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts: 9:16, 1080 x 1920.
  • YouTube and website banners: 16:9, 1920 x 1080 or higher.

File format for images

PNG keeps edges crisp and supports transparent backgrounds, so it is ideal when you want to place the model on your own banner. JPG gives much smaller files and is fine for photographic backgrounds. WebP offers a good balance for websites. Avoid saving the same JPG repeatedly, because each save adds compression artefacts.

Consistency builds trust

Use the same background, lighting and camera distance across all your listings. A shop whose previews look uniform feels organised and reliable. Add the model name and its real size as a caption when you can; buyers want to know whether a figure is 5 cm or 25 cm tall before they spend money and filament.

Do not misrepresent the print

A rendered preview shows the digital model, not your printer's result. Label renders clearly and add real photos of the physical print when you have them. Honest previews reduce complaints and refund requests, and they protect your reputation over the long term.
