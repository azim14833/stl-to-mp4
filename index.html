Free Online STL to MP4 Converter

STLtoMP4.com turns 3D models (STL, 3MF and OBJ) into smooth MP4 turntable videos and clean view images – front, back, left, right, top, bottom and isometric – directly in your browser. It is built for makers who want attractive previews of their 3D prints for Cults3D, Thingiverse, Printables, MakerWorld, Etsy, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, without installing software or uploading private files.

How to convert STL to MP4

  1. Drop your .STL, .3MF, .OBJ or .PLY file into the preview area or press Open model.
  2. Pick a scene, material, colour and camera angle. Plastic, glossy, matte, clay and metal finishes are included.
  3. Choose resolution, frame rate and video length in the Output tab.
  4. Press Create MP4 + views and download the video and images.

Frequently asked questions

Is my 3D file uploaded to any server?

No. Conversion runs entirely inside your browser with WebGL, so your STL, 3MF and OBJ files never leave your device.

Which file formats are supported?

STL (binary and ASCII), 3MF, OBJ and PLY, including colored models. You can load several models and process them one after another.

What can I export?

MP4 turntable videos (up to 4K where your device allows it), view images (front, back, left, right, top, bottom, isometric) as PNG, JPG or WebP, an optional animated GIF and a contact sheet.

Why is my model tiny or the volume 0?

STL files have no unit. Choose the file unit (mm, cm, m, inch) or type the real longest edge in millimetres in the Model tab and the volume and weight estimate update.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in Chrome on Android and tap Install app, or use the browser menu and choose Install app. It then opens full screen and works offline after the first visit.

Is STLtoMP4 free?

Yes, it is free for personal and commercial use. The site is supported by ads.
